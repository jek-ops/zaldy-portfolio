Style guide

Clear Blueprint

Calm, structured and readable. One accent color, generous spacing, and nothing that moves without a reason.

Surface

#FFFFFF · cards, nav

Ink

Accent

#1D4ED8 · buttons, links. About 6.7:1 on white

Focus

#B45309 · keyboard focus ring only

Dark mode

Same layout, darker ground

Ground #0B1220, surface #131C31, text #F1F5F9, muted #B4C0D3. The accent lightens to #8AB0FF so it still passes contrast.

Interaction states

Filter and tab pills fill with the accent when selected. Cards lift 4 px with an accent border on hover. Motion lasts 0.15 to 0.3 seconds and switches off for people who ask for reduced motion.
